The function keeps two strings in sync. lower is a lowercased copy used only for searching. result keeps the original casing and receives the asterisks. A match is masked only when the characters on both sides are not letters. That is why "class" survives a ban on "ass".

left edge: idx == 0 or not lower[idx-1].isalpha()
right edge: end == len or not lower[end].isalpha()
